# Darcy Homepage Concept v5 — Photography-led Narrative

## Core idea

The building and the people doing the work are the primary interface. Copy supports the image; it does not compete with it.

> Every section should earn its place. If removing a section makes the homepage stronger, remove it.

## Design Principles

Every page must feel:

- Calm before busy.
- Photography before graphics.
- Space before decoration.
- Structure before animation.
- Quality before quantity.
- Confidence before marketing.
- Simplicity before complexity.

If a design decision conflicts with these principles, redesign it rather than adding another component.

## Do Not Build

The Darcy website must not include:

- Floating statistic cards.
- Generic feature boxes.
- Multiple button styles.
- Coloured background sections without purpose.
- Large icon grids.
- Carousels.
- Auto-playing animations.
- Decorative gradients.
- Stock construction imagery.
- Marketing jargon.
- Duplicate content.

Every new section must justify its inclusion.

## Page narrative

### 1. Hero — Show the quality before describing it

- Full-width Darcy-owned project image or approved video.
- One concise confidence-led headline supported by the image.
- One supporting sentence.
- Two clear actions: View Our Projects and Start a Conversation.
- No floating cards, statistics or secondary messages.

### 2. About Darcy — Establish credibility quickly

- One supporting architectural, team or site image.
- A concise statement establishing Darcy since 1999 and its regional partner positioning.
- Established 1999 shown as quiet supporting evidence.
- One Learn more link to Our Story.

### 3. Featured Projects — Make the evidence the centrepiece

- One flagship project featured large, with two supporting projects beneath.
- Sector and location metadata only where verified.
- One sentence per project.
- No card borders or boxed layouts.
- Full project links through image, title and caption.

### 4. What We Do — Services and sectors, clearly separated

- Services: Construction Management, Main Contracting, Refurbishment, Design & Build.
- Sectors: Education, Commercial, Residential, Community.
- Services and sectors presented as adjacent clean text lists with subtle dividers.
- No repeated service or sector cards.

### 5. Why Clients Choose Darcy — Can I trust the team?

- Four or five concise reasons: established since 1999, senior involvement, transparent communication, trusted regional delivery and quality without compromise.
- No icons unless they are subtle and consistently applied.

### 6. Contact — Make the next step easy

- One final approved photograph.
- “Let’s discuss your next project.”
- Phone, email and one contact button.

## Visual system

- One 84rem maximum container.
- One responsive gutter token.
- One 12-column desktop grid.
- White: 70–75% of the page.
- Darcy navy: 20–25% for navigation, footer, major headings and CTA band.
- Muted construction green: 3–5% for labels, dividers and active states.
- Gold: less than 2%, reserved for CTA emphasis and small details.
- No beige panels, gradients, floating boxes or decorative card systems.

## Typography

- Display heading: one restrained serif scale.
- Section heading: same family, smaller scale.
- Body: one readable sans-serif scale.
- Caption: one uppercase metadata style.
- No additional typography variants.

## Photography requirements

The concept should not be approved for production until Darcy supplies or approves:

- Hero project image.
- Large featured-project images.
- Construction detail image.
- People/site image.
- Leadership or client conversation image.
- Usage permissions and factual alt text.

## Approval gate

This is a concept specification only. No HTML, CSS or route implementation should continue until the photography-led direction is approved.
